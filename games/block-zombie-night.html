🧟 Block Zombie Night

How to play: Defend your base! Day: loot ammo and wood (walk over crates). Night: zombies attack — shoot with mouse / Space, build barricades with F (costs 5 wood) in front of you. Don't let base HP hit 0. R = reload. P = pause.
