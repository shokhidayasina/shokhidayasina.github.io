How to use this dictionary / Lug'atdan qanday foydalanish:
  1. Choose your language from the top-right selector / Yuqori o'ngdagi tanlovdan tilingizni tanlang.
  2. Type a word in English or in your language in the search box / Qidiruvga inglizcha yoki o'z tilingizdagi so'zni yozing.
  3. Tap a category button to filter words / So'zlarni saralash uchun kategoriya tugmasini bosing.
  4. Read the translation and example / Tarjimasi va misolni o'qing.
